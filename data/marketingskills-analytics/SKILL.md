# Marketing Skills: Analytics Tracking

Aimed at the moment someone asks how to measure something. It begins with business context and the current tracking state, then lays down principles: track for decisions rather than data, start with the questions, name things consistently, protect data quality, compute rather than eyeball, match observation windows, and recommend against a stated goal when the numbers do not support it. A tracking-plan framework turns those into events and properties. It covers GA4, tag managers, UTM conventions, and tools such as Mixpanel and Segment, and helps debug events that do not fire.

## Use it to

- Draft a tracking plan with named events and properties before implementation
- Audit why conversions in GA4 do not match what the backend recorded
- Standardise UTM parameters so campaign reports stop fragmenting
- Decide which analytics events are worth implementing and which are noise
