# PM Skills: North Star Metric

Helps you choose the single metric that best captures the value customers get, then surrounds it with three to five input metrics that form a constellation. It classifies the business game being played (attention, transaction or productivity) and validates the candidate against seven criteria. It also states what a North Star is not: several metrics, a revenue or lifetime value figure, an OKR or a strategy. A domain-context section, a prompt and tips follow. Use it when setting up a metrics framework or learning the North Star framework, and expect the model to push back on revenue-style answers.

## Use it to

- Pick a North Star Metric and the input metrics that drive it
- Identify whether the product plays an attention, transaction or productivity game
- Test a candidate metric against the validation criteria
- Replace a revenue-based goal with a customer-centric metric
