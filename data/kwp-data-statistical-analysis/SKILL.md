# Knowledge Work: Statistical Analysis

A methods reference for analysts. It covers descriptive statistics, choosing a measure of center, spread, percentiles for business contexts and describing distributions; trend analysis with seasonality detection and simple forecasting; outlier and anomaly detection with ways to handle outliers; and hypothesis testing. Throughout, it gives guidance on when to be cautious about statistical claims. It sits in Anthropic's data plugin and is intended for analysing distributions, testing significance, detecting anomalies, computing correlations or interpreting statistical results.

## Use it to

- Pick the right measure of center for a skewed business metric
- Flag anomalies in a weekly metric series with a stated method
- Run a significance test and report it with appropriate caveats
- Detect seasonality before forecasting a metric
- Check the assumptions behind a test, such as sample size and distribution shape, before trusting a p-value
