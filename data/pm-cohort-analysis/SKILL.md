# PM Skills: Cohort Analysis

Reads your engagement data, validates it, then produces quantitative analysis and visualizations of retention by cohort, feature adoption over time and long-term engagement. Five steps lead from reading and validating data to generating analysis, creating charts, identifying insights and suggesting follow-up research that combines the numbers with qualitative questions. Usage examples and an output format are provided. It suits investigating churn patterns and engagement trends when you can supply a dataset, such as an export of user events or subscriptions, and want a structured readout.

## Use it to

- Build retention curves from a user-events export
- Compare feature adoption between signup cohorts
- Investigate when and where a churn pattern begins
- Turn analysis findings into follow-up research questions
- Compare retention for users who finished onboarding against those who did not, and list hypotheses
