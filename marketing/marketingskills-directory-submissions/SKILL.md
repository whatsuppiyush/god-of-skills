# Marketing Skills: Directory Submissions

A workflow for getting a product listed across directories for backlinks, domain rating and discovery. Three hard rules lead: build the foundation before submitting, create destination pages before directories, and vary positioning by directory type. The steps are a readiness assessment, choosing tiers, preparing asset variations, batch submitting and verifying the actual backlink. Deep dives cover a Product Hunt launch, with a three-week prep timeline, and a reviews playbook for G2, Capterra and TrustRadius built around getting ten reviews in thirty days. It also advises on which pages the links should point at.

## Use it to

- Prepare a tiered list of directories and the assets each one needs
- Plan a Product Hunt launch with a three-week preparation timeline
- Run a review-collection push for G2 and Capterra
- Check that submitted listings actually produced a live backlink
