# LinkedIn Skills: Hook Extractor

Paste the URL of a viral LinkedIn post and the skill identifies which of the twenty canonical formulas it uses, such as anaphora, year-pivot, comment-gate, emotional cold-open or named gratitude. It explains why the hook worked, shows the exact structure and returns a blank template mapped to your own topic. Because fetched posts are untrusted content, a dedicated section tells the model not to follow instructions embedded in them. It is a study tool rather than a writing tool, and it hands off to the post-writer skill when you want to draft.

## Use it to

- Analyze why a competitor's post went viral and what structure it used
- Build a swipe file of hooks organised by formula
- Convert a viral post's structure into a template for your topic
- Learn the formula library by dissecting real examples
