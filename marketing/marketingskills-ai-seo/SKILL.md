# Marketing Skills: AI SEO

A long playbook for answer-engine optimization, written for the case where you want to be cited by language models as well as ranked by Google. It begins with an assessment of your current AI visibility, content and domain, goals and competitors. It then explains how AI search differs from traditional SEO and where Google's stated position diverges from the multi-platform reality. Reference files go deeper on topics such as whether agents can read your site at all, bot access and discovery files, and measuring delayed visits from AI answers through branded search and pipeline.

## Use it to

- Audit whether AI crawlers can actually read and extract your key pages
- Restructure an article so passages are quotable by answer engines
- Set up a way to measure AI-driven traffic that does not show up as a referral
- Compare how your brand appears across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews

**Claude Code**
