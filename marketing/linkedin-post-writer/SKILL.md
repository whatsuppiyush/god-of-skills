# LinkedIn Skills: Post Writer

Writes a LinkedIn post from scratch using one of twenty named hook formulas from the 2026 set, including anaphora, R.I.P., time-anchor, curiosity-gap, contrarian and false-binary, plus an angle library aimed at founders. The formula is picked by goal first: comments, reposts, likes or saves. Steps include a humanizer pass and optional scheduling through Publora. The skill records 2026 reach caveats from an audit, states hard rules taken from user feedback and lists anti-patterns it refuses to write. It is a longer skill of over two thousand words with resource files.

## Use it to

- Draft a long-form post tuned for comments rather than likes
- Choose a hook formula to match an engagement goal
- Write a founder-voice post from a rough idea
- Schedule the finished post through a connected scheduler
- Draft a second version of the same idea with a different formula and compare the two
