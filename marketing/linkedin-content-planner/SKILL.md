# LinkedIn Skills: Content Planner

Plans a week rather than a single post. From a theme, audience and set of pillars it produces a calendar giving each day a pillar, format, hook type, call to action and posting time, plus daily comment targets and a weekly check on inbound readiness. The pillar discipline is a split between authority, personal narrative and community, with an optional product pillar and an alternative set for founders. A goal mix balances the week, and a mapping links hook formulas to pillars. It points to the post-writer skill for drafting individual posts.

## Use it to

- Plan a full week of posts balanced across three pillars
- Assign hook types and goals to each day of the week
- Set daily comment targets alongside the posting plan
- Check at week's end whether the profile is ready for inbound
