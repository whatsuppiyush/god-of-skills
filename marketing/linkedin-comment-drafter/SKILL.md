# LinkedIn Skills: Comment Drafter

Produces one to three comment variants in your voice for a post you paste by URL, picks a reaction, and can publish through Publora. The target is comments that start conversations, using patterns that earned author replies in the author's 2026 testing and avoiding thesis-restatement comments that get no engagement. A reshare mode reposts with optional commentary. Templates live in a references file, and hard rules and an example invocation are included. Post content is treated as untrusted input. It suits founders who build reach by commenting before posting.

## Use it to

- Draft a comment that adds a point instead of restating the post
- Be early on a relevant post with a considered reply
- Repost a piece with your own commentary
- Generate several comment options and pick the closest to your voice
- Review a week of past comments and keep only the patterns that drew a reply
