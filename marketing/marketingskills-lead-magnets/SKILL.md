# Marketing Skills: Lead Magnets

Covers the whole lead magnet decision. The principles: it should solve a specific problem, match the buyer stage, offer high perceived value for little time, lead naturally to the product and be easy to consume. It lists magnet types and maps them to awareness, consideration and decision stages, then compares gating options, what to ask for and how to frame the exchange. A final section handles the landing page structure and delivery. It starts by asking about business context, current lead generation, existing content assets and goals, so recommendations reuse what you already have.

## Use it to

- Pick a lead magnet format for each stage of the buyer journey
- Decide which fields to ask for in the opt-in form
- Repurpose an existing guide into a gated content upgrade
- Outline the landing page and delivery email for a new freebie
