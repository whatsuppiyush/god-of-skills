# Marketing Skills: Churn Prevention

Splits churn into two problems, voluntary cancellations and involuntary ones caused by failed payments, and gives each its own toolkit. For the first it designs the cancel flow, an exit survey and dynamic save offers matched to the reason given. For the second it covers dunning and payment recovery. The skill begins by asking for the current churn rate, billing platform, product and usage data, and constraints. It also touches pause options, win-back and offboarding. It suits a SaaS or subscription team that knows churn is a problem but has no structured response beyond a cancel button.

## Use it to

- Build a cancel flow that asks why and presents a relevant save offer
- Design a dunning sequence for failed card payments
- Write an exit survey whose answers can feed product decisions
- Add a pause-subscription option and decide when to show it
