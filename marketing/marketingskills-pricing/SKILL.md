# Marketing Skills: Pricing Strategy

Pricing guidance that gets specific. It explains the three pricing axes and value-based pricing, advises picking a first price you can learn from using a $10, $100, $1,000 rule of thumb, warns about the $9 trap, and includes a section on charging $50 and seeing what happens. Sections cover value metrics, good-better-best tier structures, research methods such as Van Westendorp and MaxDiff, signs that it is time to raise prices, rollout methodology for increases, and what belongs above the fold on a pricing page. Freemium and trial decisions are included.

## Use it to

- Choose a value metric that scales with the customer's usage
- Draft good, better and best tiers with clear differentiation
- Plan and roll out a price increase to existing customers
- Run a Van Westendorp survey to find an acceptable price range
- Prepare customer interview questions that test willingness to pay before you change any plan
