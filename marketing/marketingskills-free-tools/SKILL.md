# Marketing Skills: Free Tools Strategy

Treats a free tool as a marketing investment to be screened before anyone builds it. The principles are that it must solve a real problem, sit next to the core product, stay simple and focused, and be worth the build cost. An ideation framework starts from customer pain points and validation steps. A lead capture strategy follows, comparing gating options and best practices, along with SEO considerations for the tool's page. It covers ROI calculators, graders, generators and other interactive tools. The output is a decision about what to build, not the tool itself.

## Use it to

- Score a list of free tool ideas against the four principles before building any
- Decide how much of a tool's output to gate behind an email
- Plan the search landing page for a calculator
- Pick a tool idea that complements rather than substitutes for the paid product
