# Marketing Skills: Launch Strategy

Organizes channels into owned, rented and borrowed under the ORB framework, then checks a readiness gate before any announcement. The five-phase approach runs from an internal launch through alpha, beta and early access to a full launch, so momentum builds rather than spikes once. A Product Hunt section weighs the pros and cons and explains how to launch successfully, with case studies. Post-launch guidance covers immediate actions and how to keep momentum going. It works for new products, major features, waitlists, beta releases and site launches, and ends with an ongoing launch cadence.

## Use it to

- Sequence a staged launch from internal testing through general availability
- Decide whether Product Hunt is worth the effort for a given product
- Map which owned, rented and borrowed channels to use at launch
- Write the checklist of things to do in the week after the announcement
