# Knowledge Work: Email Sequence

Builds a whole lifecycle flow rather than a single message. The process covers sequence strategy, individual email design including full copy, sequence logic such as timing, branching and exit conditions, and performance benchmarks. Templates exist for sequence types such as onboarding, lead nurture, re-engagement, win-back and product launch, and it suggests A/B tests. If an email marketing platform such as Klaviyo or Mailchimp is connected, or a marketing automation tool or CRM, it adapts to them. Triggered by /email-sequence or by asking for a drip campaign or nurture flow.

## Use it to

- Write a five-email onboarding sequence with branching on user actions
- Design a win-back flow with clear exit conditions
- Map timing and benchmarks for a lead nurture series
- Suggest A/B tests for subject lines and send times
- Compare the proposed timing against your current open and click benchmarks before building it
