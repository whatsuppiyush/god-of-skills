# Organic Social

A system for earning reach and building an audience on social platforms with no ad budget. It
covers short-form video mechanics, per-platform algorithm rules, content-production systems, and
cold-start growth, so you can diagnose why posts underperform and pick the right lever.

## When to use this

- Scripting or editing short-form video (Reels, Shorts, TikTok) and want more watch-through.
- Posting on a specific platform (X, LinkedIn, Reddit, Instagram) and want to work its algorithm.
- Starting from near-zero followers and stuck in the cold-start loop (no followers, no reach).
- Drowning in "what do I post today" or trying to squeeze more output from one recording.
- Deciding whether organic content is even worth the time, or whether to chase a new platform.
- Symptoms: "my posts get no reach", "views but no followers", "I ran out of ideas", "how do I go
  viral", "should I be on Threads/[new platform]", "how do I grow my LinkedIn/X".

## When NOT to use this (reach for instead)

- **Paying creators to post for you** (gifting, affiliate deals, UGC army, seeding, briefs,
  whitelisting): use `influencer-creator`. Organic-social is what YOU post; influencer-creator is
  what you pay OTHERS to post. The two pair: validate a hook organically, then hand winners to
  creators.
- **The actual words/hooks/copy** inside a post or script: use `copywriting-messaging` for the line
  craft; this skill decides structure, format, timing, and distribution.
- **Turning organic winners into paid ads** (ad accounts, targeting, CAC/ROAS on spend): use
  `paid-acquisition`. Organic is the free creative-testing lab; paid amplifies the winners.

## How this works (decision path)

1. **Decide if organic is your channel at all.** If unproven, run the content-investment test
   (10+ hrs/week, 6-month horizon, a kill metric) before going deep → `content-creation-commitment`.
2. **Diagnose by follower size.** Near-zero and no reach is a cold-start problem, not a content
   problem → start with `cold-start-follower-growth`. Interest-based platforms (TikTok) let a new
   account break out on content quality alone → `tiktok-organic-first-validation`.
3. **Pick the platform and write to its rules.** Each algorithm rewards different signals. Go to the
   matching platform play below before drafting.
4. **If it's video, engineer the structure.** Hook in the first seconds, hold retention, loop the
   ending, place the CTA mid-video. Structure beats budget.
5. **Solve idea supply with a system.** One idea becomes 10+ posts; one recording becomes a week of
   multichannel content. Don't create each post from scratch.
6. **Layer reactive + breakout plays on top.** Trendjacking borrows existing attention; the breakout
   playbooks are high-variance commitments when incremental tactics aren't enough.

## Key numbers and benchmarks

**Video / short-form**
- Hook in the first 3 seconds; state the value in the first 5 seconds and leave a question open until
  the final 5. Design the ending to loop so retention passes 100%.
- Instagram limits: Stories 15s/card, Reels 60s (test under vs over 30s), feed autoplay preview 60s.
  Story ads 5–15s, Reels ads up to 30s, feed ads up to 60s. Shorter ads (~30s) usually win.
- Peak-end rule: the last ~30 seconds are weighted disproportionately in how viewers rate a video.
- Thumbnails: big non-rounded numbers ("$1,247" beats "$1,000"), a genuine reaction, a question the
  title answers.

**X / Twitter**
- Boosts: media 2×, replying to your own post 2×, Twitter Blue 4× to followers / 2× to non-followers.
- Penalties: language mismatch −90%, multiple hashtags −40%, misspellings −95%, off-platform links.
- Keep following ≤60% of your follower count to protect TweepCred. ~50% of engagement lands in hour 1.
- Time by type: educational before 4 PM, entertainment after 4 PM.

**LinkedIn**
- Default organic reach is under 10% of follower count; comments are the primary virality lever
  (a comment can 4× a like's reach). Posts of 500+ words tend to travel further.
- 500+ connections earns the "500+" badge. Go 28 days without posting and the next post is demoted.
- Automation safety rail: ~20 connection requests/day (Dux-Soup ~80/day, ~20% below the 100 cap).
  Email-capture conversion is low: a 100k-follower creator converted ~5% to newsletter.

**Reddit**
- Karma Ladder: ~100 karma in non-business subs (30 days) → ~60 value-only industry comments
  (60 days) → conversation-starter threads (target 100k+ impressions consumer, 25k+ B2B) → mods/AMAs.
- Budget ~2–4 hrs/week over ~3 months. Threads also win SEO real estate on brand-name searches.

**Cold-start / growth**
- Follow-to-enter giveaway CPA can hit ~$0.004 (vs ~$2 Twitter benchmark), but low-intent followers.
- 80/20 niche ratio until ~15–20k followers, then add ~20% adjacent value content.
- Content-investment test: 10+ hrs/week, 6 months, a kill metric; split 40% create / 30% edit /
  20% distribute / 10% analyze. Claim-and-watch new platforms for 2–3 months before investing.

### Grow a Twitter/LinkedIn audience 1k→60k in a year with an 80/20 niche ratio and direct-response posts

## The strategy
A case-study teardown of how Amanda Natividad (SparkToro) grew Twitter from 1k → 60k in a year (now 100k+), plus 12k on LinkedIn and a 5k newsletter. The repeatable engine: stay ruthlessly in-niche until you have a base, apply direct-response copywriting to every post, and treat "audience building" as networking at scale.

## When to use it
Solo operators, founders, and marketers building a personal audience from a small following.

## How to execute (steps)
1. **Define why you're posting before you post.** Her goals evolved (employer brand → niche recognition → career growth without a job search → newsletter monetization). Purpose sets the content.
2. **Hold an 80/20 niche ratio until ~15-20k followers.** First 6-10 months: ~80% marketing/content-strategy content only. After ~15k, add ~20% adjacent content (recipes, relatable anecdotes), but every off-niche post must still deliver value.
3. **Write posts like direct-response copy:**
   - Deliver value in the opening line; active-voice verbs ("clarify," "learn," "get"), no passive construction.
   - Use curiosity-gap hooks with specificity, e.g. "14 marketing tools you (probably) haven't heard of yet."
   - Cut context ruthlessly; end threads with a clear CTA.
4. **Mine FAQs.** Repeated questions from engaged followers signal scalable content topics.
5. **Filter for value over virality.** Reject high-engagement post ideas that don't fit your positioning.
6. **Leverage off-platform:** 100+ podcast appearances during the growth year; convert blog posts into threads and threads into blog posts; actually test tactics (e.g. run email campaigns) so you can write about them credibly.
7. **Contribute to community:** reply on others' posts, amplify underrepresented voices, give unpaid help (job tools, course scholarships).
8. **Avoid trust-killers:** plagiarism and "bro math" (fake stats like "99.3447% of tweets").

## Notes / caveats / examples
- Verified numbers: Twitter 1k→60k in 1yr (now 100k+); LinkedIn 12k; newsletter (The Menu) 5k; a course waitlist hit 300 signups in 2 days. Threads regularly drew 4-10k+ likes; one LinkedIn post on overqualification got 14k reactions / 1.1M impressions. She targeted ~1,000 likes per post.
- She applied classic direct-response copywriting principles to every social post.
- Reframe: audience building = networking at scale, not status-seeking.

## → Skill conversion note
Becomes an `audience-growth-plan` prompt: input niche + current following, output the 80/20 content ratio, a direct-response post template with hook formula, an FAQ-mining loop, and an off-platform (podcast/blog↔thread) repurposing schedule.

### Three breakout 0-to-mass playbooks, gamified escalating stakes, raw daily authenticity, and SEO-style niche ideation

## The strategy
Three documented cases where creators went from near-zero to massive in weeks-to-months, each via a distinct, copyable mechanism: gamifying growth with escalating stakes, leaning into raw authenticity over production, and treating content ideation like SEO. Pick the one that fits your personality and product.

## When to use it
When you want a proven, high-variance growth model to commit to, not incremental tactics. Each demands consistency and a clear point of view.

## How to execute (steps)
1. **Gamified escalating stakes (Chewy Thompson: 520 → 128k followers in 8 days):** tie audience actions to escalating personal stakes, 1 follower = 1 inch walked/day, 1,000 followers = 1 pushup/day, and add a deadline threat ("delete channel unless 1M in 365 days"). The hero's-journey framing plus rising stakes makes following feel consequential.
2. **Raw daily authenticity (Sam Sulek: 8k → 2.75M in 9 months):** post daily, minimally-edited 30-40 min vlogs with plain thumbnails/titles, casual settings, no "creator voice," and community inside jokes. The lack of polish *is* the differentiator.
3. **SEO-style niche ideation (Nat Eliason: 400 → 55,900 followers in 6 weeks on TikTok):** study the top 5-10 accounts in your niche, hook in the first 2-3 seconds (repeat the hook in the description), edit on desktop, and skip trends/comment games, treat topics like keywords.
4. **Commit to one model** and run it long enough to compound, these are systems, not single posts.

## Notes / caveats / examples
- The common thread: a single, legible mechanic executed relentlessly, stakes, authenticity, or niche-keyword ideation, not a mix of everything.
- Sulek's case is the counter to over-production: chasing polish can actively hurt when the audience wants realness.

## → Skill conversion note
Becomes a "breakout-playbook-picker" SKILL.md: input creator personality + product + platform, recommend which of the three models fits, and generate the first month's plan (stakes ladder, vlog cadence, or niche-keyword content calendar).

### Get from zero to first followers, warm targets before connecting, work follower thresholds, and buy cheap followers with a follow-to-enter giveaway

## The strategy
The hardest part of any account is the cold start, no followers means no reach means no followers. Break the loop with three tactics keyed to your current size: warm up bigger accounts by engaging before you connect, follow threshold-specific playbooks, and use a follow-to-enter giveaway to buy followers at a fraction of ad CPA.

## When to use it
A brand-new or stalled account (roughly under 100k followers) that needs to manufacture its first traction before the algorithm does the work for it.

## How to execute (steps)
1. **Warm before you connect (LinkedIn bootstrapping):** target accounts ~3× your size, comment on their viral posts over days/weeks so you're recognized, then send connection requests, cap ~20/day to avoid suspension.
2. **Work the follower thresholds (1k / 5k / 10k / 50k / 100k):** under 100 followers, recruit personal contacts; then DM engagers, comment on same-size creators to trade audiences, and reply on big creators' posts; secure reposts. Use LinkedIn automation (WeConnect, Lemlist) for 20-30 daily connects.
3. **Run a follow-to-enter giveaway** for cheap follower CPA, MrBeast spent $50k for 12.5M followers = $0.004 CPA vs. Twitter's ~$2 benchmark. Mark it as not platform-endorsed to avoid removal.
4. **Graduate tactics as you grow**, what works at 500 followers (DMing engagers) differs from 50k (securing reposts from peers).

## Notes / caveats / examples
- The giveaway CPA gap is enormous, $0.004 vs ~$2, but giveaway followers are lower-intent, so pair with value content to retain them.
- The 20-connects/day cap on LinkedIn is a hard safety rail; exceeding it risks account suspension.

## → Skill conversion note
Becomes a "cold-start-growth" SKILL.md: input current follower count + platform, output the threshold-appropriate playbook (warm-up targets, engagement quotas, giveaway mechanics with CPA math) and daily action limits to stay within platform rules.

### Maximize algorithmic reach with zero-click content and comment-gating, deliver full value in-post, keep links out, and DM the URL

## The strategy
Social algorithms suppress content that pushes people off-platform and reward content that keeps them there. So deliver the full value inside the post (zero-click), publish on a fixed schedule to farm early comments, and when you must share a link, comment-gate it, ask people to reply, then DM the URL, instead of posting the link and eating the reach penalty.

## When to use it
Any organic post where you'd instinctively drop a link, lead magnets, launches, newsletters, courses, and any account where consistency + early engagement can be engineered.

## How to execute (steps)
1. **Publish zero-click content:** deliver complete value in-post; make links optional, not required. Algorithms favor native content over clickbait (Amanda Natividad's principle).
2. **Post on a fixed schedule and invite immediate comments**, early comments boost algorithmic reach (Justin Welsh: 300k+ followers, $3M solo revenue on this rhythm).
3. **Comment-gate links instead of posting them:** ask readers to "reply with 👀" (or a keyword), then DM the URL. This dodges link suppression and the reply volume itself triggers viral feed spread.
4. **Optimize for intention, not attention:** measure leads/DMs/replies over likes/followers, and define the "job to be done" each post solves.

## Notes / caveats / examples
- Comment-gating result: 500+ waitlist signups in 24 hours and a third of course sales ($50K+) from a single gated post.
- The mechanism behind comment-gating is double: it avoids the off-platform link penalty *and* the flood of replies is itself a strong engagement signal.
- "Intention over attention" reframes the goal, a post with 50 DMs beats a post with 5,000 likes and no leads.

## → Skill conversion note
Becomes a "native-reach" SKILL.md: input a piece of content + its link/CTA, output a zero-click rewrite that carries full value in-post plus a comment-gating mechanic (trigger phrase + DM flow) and an intention-based metric to track.

### Decide whether to invest in content creation, run a 6-month test with a kill metric, a fixed time split, and a wait-and-see rule for new platforms

## The strategy
Content creation is a real resource bet, so treat it like an experiment with a defined budget, a kill metric, and a disciplined time allocation, not an open-ended "let's try posting." And when a shiny new platform appears, claim your spot but wait to see if it's worth real investment before pouring effort in.

## When to use it
Before committing to organic content as a channel, when justifying content ROI against paid, or when deciding whether to chase a newly-hyped platform.

## How to execute (steps)
1. **Commit 10+ hrs/week for a 6-month test with a kill metric**, decide upfront what result would make you stop.
2. **Split the time 40% create / 30% edit / 20% distribute / 10% analyze**, distribution and analysis are where most people underinvest.
3. **Master one platform first**, then apply 80/20: 80% proven format, 20% experiment.
4. **Compare organic ROI honestly to paid**, a $5K polished video with minimal engagement loses to $5K of FB ads at $0.89 CPC ≈ 5,600 visits; but Kevin DePopas's 30-min LinkedIn post got 826 comments + a signup spike, beating a produced video. Judge by outcomes, not production cost.
5. **Wait-and-see on new platforms:** claim the handle + bio + link + one placeholder post, then monitor 2-3 months before investing. Threads hit 100M users in 5 days but with unclear value.

## Notes / caveats / examples
- The 40/30/20/10 split is the corrective to "just post more", creating without distributing/analyzing wastes the effort.
- The DePopas vs polished-video comparison is the core lesson: engagement and outcomes matter, not budget or polish.

## → Skill conversion note
Becomes a "content-investment-planner" SKILL.md: input available hours + goal, output a 6-month test plan (kill metric, time-split schedule, platform choice) and a new-platform triage checklist (claim-and-watch vs invest).

### Match Instagram video length to format, 15s Story cards, sub-30s Reels/ads, 60s feed previews

## The strategy
Each Instagram surface has a hard length limit and a different optimal duration. Cutting to the right length (and hooking in the first frame) is what drives watch-through and distribution.

## When to use it
Producing or repurposing video for Instagram and deciding how to cut it per placement.

## How to execute (steps)
**Format limits and optimal lengths:**
- **Stories:** 15s max per card. Videos under 60s auto-split into 4 × 15s cards; content past 60s is discarded unless manually trimmed. Photos display 7s each. Best for engaging existing followers.
- **Reels:** 60s max. Test under-30s vs. over-30s. Best for reaching new audiences, strong engagement earns expanded distribution. Capture attention in the first 3 seconds (most swipes happen in the opening seconds).
- **Live:** up to 4 hours. Length scales with audience (celebrity streams run ~115 min; a focused educational Q&A ~35 min). Pre-announce with countdowns/teasers.
- **Feed video (ex-IGTV):** 15s, 10 min for regular accounts, up to 60 min for verified/large. The in-feed autoplay preview is 60s, keep to that preview window to minimize friction.
- **Video ads:** Story ads 5-15s; Reels ads up to 30s; feed ads up to 60s. Shorter ads (~30s) typically perform best.

**Production tactics:**
- Invest testing budget in the first frame / hook, it decides watch-through.
- Edit ruthlessly: "remove every frame that isn't necessary, no lulls, only action."
- Design for muted playback; caption key messaging; layer 4-6 quick cuts per Story card for variety.
- Shoot native vertical; blend ads into organic with popular effects/animations/audio.
- For longer Story sequences, open with a binary yes/no poll (micro-commitment) and alternate education cards with polls/quizzes.
- Unboxing watch-time sequence: package pickup → transport → cut open → inspect → assemble → reveal.
- Convert top-performing Story videos into permanent profile Highlights.

## Notes / caveats / examples
- Benchmarks: Glossier 10s Reel got ~9,000 likes + 100+ comments; carousels outperformed feed videos across HubSpot's 2022 analysis of 110M posts; mobile-shot creative has a 63% chance of beating studio-shot for purchases; 92% of consumers trust UGC more than traditional ads.
- Cross-platform Story limits for reference: Facebook 20s, Snapchat 60s, WhatsApp Status 30s.
- Post timing: evenings/nights, strongest Sat, Sun, Mon; use "Most Active Times" in Insights for your account.

## → Skill conversion note
Becomes an `instagram-cut-planner` prompt: input a raw video length + goal (awareness vs. followers vs. ad), output the target placement, exact cut length, hook plan, and caption/Highlight steps.

### LinkedIn network growth, profile setup, and email capture

## The strategy
Grow reach by concentrating connections in tight persona clusters (the algorithm then promotes your posts to similar people), switch your profile to a follower model, and convert audience to email, carefully, because conversion is low.

## How to execute (steps)
- Cluster-connect: target one job-function + location cluster at a time (all Directors of Engineering in SF, then Mobile Developers in SF) so engagement concentrates and the algorithm spreads posts to similar personas.
- Send connection requests WITHOUT a personalized note (higher acceptance than templated messages); bootstrap by importing Gmail/company contacts; build toward the 30,000 connection cap strategically.
- Automate with Dux-Soup (set 80 auto-connections/day, ~20% below the 100/day limit) or Botdog / Phantom Buster / We-connect; LinkedIn doesn't currently penalize connection bots.
- Enable the Follow button as primary: Settings & Privacy > Visibility > Followers > "Make follow primary" (followers are unlimited and see content immediately vs the 30k connection cap).
- Optimize the profile: bio header shows social proof (YC, TechCrunch, Forbes 30u30, investor status) and appears on outgoing requests; About = one sentence on your value to the audience + supporting proof, kept short.
- Engage to grow: comment on 2-3 influencer posts daily (top comments on viral posts expose you), send connection requests to everyone who likes/comments your posts, test ideas in comments then repurpose winners as posts.
- Capture email: post freely 4 of 5 times; every 5th post give half the content and gate the rest behind email; DM offers (newsletters, VC/recruiter lists, guides) only after 30+ days of insightful posting.

## Notes / caveats / examples
- Posting discipline: go 28 days without posting and your next post gets "heavily demoted."
- Email-capture benchmark: a top creator with 100k+ followers converted only 5% to newsletter subscribers.

## → Skill conversion note
A "LinkedIn growth planner" skill: given a target persona, output cluster segments, a connection cadence, profile-copy rewrites, and a 5-post gated-content schedule.

### Grow a LinkedIn presence by optimizing the profile and engineering comments (not likes) for organic reach

## The strategy
On LinkedIn, default organic reach is under 10% of your follower count, and comments, not likes or reshares, are the primary lever for virality (per AJ Wilcox, B2Linked). Growth = a conversion-optimized profile plus long-form, comment-provoking content in a narrow niche.

## When to use it
B2B founders/marketers building a personal brand or thought-leadership channel. LinkedIn connections convert to B2B customers better than Twitter equivalents because of same-industry intent.

## How to execute (steps)
1. **Profile optimization:**
   - Pass 500+ connections to earn the "500+" badge (masks your real count, signals a strong network).
   - Header image must grab attention instantly: speaking photos, team shots, or graphics with a CTA/social proof.
   - Headline (shown before profile click) should create intrigue: recognizable credentials (e.g. "Forbes 30 Under 30"), an unusual narrative ("former monk, current business coach"), or notable projects.
   - About section: keep it succinct, list only impressive/relevant accomplishments, and add outbound link cards to break up text and lift engagement.
2. **Pick a narrow niche.** Specific beats broad (e.g. "email marketing strategy for e-commerce"). Susan Cain built 2M followers on introvert content alone.
3. **Write for comments and depth:**
   - Posts of 500+ words tend to go more viral, depth over brevity (opposite of Twitter).
   - Share trending articles with a contrarian spin; the Trending Topics section can distribute you beyond your network.
   - Use vulnerable, business-appropriate personal stories.
4. **Source ideas** from BuzzSumo, Google News, and Twitter keyword searches; study competitors' engagement patterns, posting frequency, and format performance.
5. **Scale outreach carefully:** tools like Botdog or Dux-Soup can send personalized connection requests at scale, but high rejection rates from irrelevant adds can trigger account restrictions.

## Notes / caveats / examples
- Hard numbers: organic reach <10% of followers; 500+ word posts favored; 500+ connections for the badge.
- Comments are "THE way to get organic virality on LinkedIn", design posts to provoke replies, not just likes.

## → Skill conversion note
Becomes a `linkedin-growth-audit` prompt: input a profile URL + niche, output profile fixes (header/headline/about), a comment-bait post template at 500+ words, and an idea-sourcing checklist.

### LinkedIn post formula, hooks, formats, and comment-driven virality

## The strategy
LinkedIn has more content demand than supply, so organic reach is unusually easy, posts routinely reach far beyond your follower base (one 7,000-follower account reached 550,000 views on a single post). Comments are the primary virality signal; the hook decides whether anyone reads.

## When to use it
Any founder/operator building B2B audience and email pipeline via organic LinkedIn.

## How to execute (steps)
- Structure a pure-text post: (1) Hook = first 2 sentences (LinkedIn truncates here), an unbelievable stat/quote, a startling personal failure, a challenge to the status quo, or a resource statement; (2) Context expanding the hook; (3) Zinger, a crisp insight that earns likes/shares.
- Use three formats: pure-text posts; resource posts (hook + context + a visual asset that carries the insight); story posts (lesson learned the hard way, funny/ironic observation, vulnerability), story posts are ~33% of top creators' content.
- Optimize for comments: comments 4x view count vs likes (a commenter's followers see it). Always end with a CTA: "Leave a comment if…", "How often does this happen to you?"
- Mobile-first formatting: ~60% of LinkedIn traffic is mobile, use 1-2 sentence paragraphs only.
- Post when most of your audience is active (typically ~8:30am their timezone).

## Notes / caveats / examples
- Posts stay visible 48-96 hours before peaking and can sustain engagement up to a month.
- Links no longer penalized (changed May 2020), but linking away reduces on-platform engagement, which reduces reach, only link if the post will still engage strongly.

## → Skill conversion note
A "LinkedIn post drafter" skill: take a raw idea and output a hook + context + zinger draft in the chosen format, mobile-formatted, with a comment-baiting CTA.

### Place the CTA mid-video at peak action (not the end) and re-hook continuously so mid-scroll arrivals stay

## The strategy
Two counterintuitive short-form moves from Jenny Hoyos (1.6B views / 124 videos, ~13M avg): put the CTA in the middle at peak action rather than the end, and re-hook the audience continuously because most viewers arrive mid-scroll, not at the start.

## When to use it
Any short-form video where you need an action (follow, comment, click), and any video long enough that a viewer might land partway through.

## How to execute (steps)
1. **Place the CTA mid-video, at the moment of peak action**, not after the payoff. Asking at the end catches viewers right as they bounce; asking mid-action catches them fully engaged and signals re-watchability.
2. **Re-hook continuously**, assume every viewer arrived mid-scroll: recap the concept repeatedly so late arrivals catch up.
3. **Add drama throughout** via dialogue, mistakes, or music shifts to reset attention.
4. **Restate the premise right before the reveal** so the payoff lands even for someone who joined seconds ago.
5. **End abruptly at the peak**, don't wind down; cut while attention is highest to maximize loops and rewatches.

## Notes / caveats / examples
- Case: Jenny Hoyos's YouTube Shorts, 1.6B views across 124 videos (~13M average).
- The mechanism: a post-payoff CTA competes with the viewer's urge to swipe; a peak-action CTA rides the engagement wave instead of fighting it.

## → Skill conversion note
Becomes a "video-cta-placement" SKILL.md: input a script or shot list, output the recommended mid-video CTA insertion point (at peak action), re-hook recap lines, and an abrupt-cut ending.

### Farm earned media reactively, social listening, trend/drama remixing ("cult hopping"), and community outreach as outbound

## The strategy
Some of the cheapest reach comes from reacting to what's already happening, not originating it. Monitor for moments where your brand can insert itself (social listening), remix competitor drama and trends into derivative content ("cult hopping"), and treat engaged communities as an outbound channel. Reactive content borrows an existing audience's attention.

## When to use it
Ongoing, as an always-on layer alongside planned content, especially when a competitor stumbles, a relevant news moment breaks, or a trend is peaking.

## How to execute (steps)
1. **Social listening as a growth channel:** watch for viral posts and competitor missteps you can respond to helpfully or opportunistically. Airbnb replied to Alix Earle's viral Booking.com villa-scam post with a luxury villa → 20M views, ~$100k earned media; ConvertKit offered free migration when Gumroad raised prices.
2. **"Cult hopping", trend/drama remixing:** build derivative content off competitor drama or a peaking trend. joefromyoutube hit 16.4M reach with a parody at only 94.6K followers; Olipop farmed free exposure commenting on Poppi's backlash. Target ≥1 trend per month.
3. **Community outreach as outbound sales:** DM community leaders *after* genuinely consuming their content; offer free accounts + member discounts instead of affiliate commissions.
4. **Move fast**, reactive plays have a short window; the value decays as the moment passes.

## Notes / caveats / examples

### Structure short-form video with a 5-second value hook, a 5-part retention skeleton, and a seamless loop
