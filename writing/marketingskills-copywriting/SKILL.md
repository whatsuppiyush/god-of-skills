# Marketing Skills: Copywriting

Writes page copy after diagnosing the situation: page purpose, audience, product and offer, and context. A drafting protocol follows. The principles are specific: clarity over cleverness, benefits over features, specificity over vagueness, the customer's language over the company's, one idea per section, and claims that can be supported. Writing style rules include a quick quality check, and a dedicated section lists AI tells to avoid, those to keep rare, and a pre-delivery check. Use it for headlines, calls to action, taglines and hero sections.

## Use it to

- Write a homepage hero and supporting sections from a product brief
- Rewrite a pricing page so each tier explains who it is for
- Draft call-to-action and headline options and pick one with reasons
- Review generated copy against the skill's list of AI tells before it ships
- Compare two headline directions on a pricing page and explain which one is more specific
