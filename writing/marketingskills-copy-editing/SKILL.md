# Marketing Skills: Copy Editing

Systematic editing for copy that already exists. Rather than fixing everything at once, it makes seven passes, each on a single dimension: clarity, voice and tone, so what, prove it, specificity, heightened emotion and zero risk. Splitting the work this way catches problems that disappear when you try to fix all of them in one read. An expert-panel scoring step rates the result. Principles include preserving the core message and using brand voice and customer language from your product marketing context when one exists. It also handles refreshing outdated pages.

## Use it to

- Run a homepage through seven sweeps and keep a log of what changed
- Check whether every claim has proof or a specific detail behind it
- Tighten wordy product page copy without changing its message
- Score two versions of a page with the expert panel to choose one
