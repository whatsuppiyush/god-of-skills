# Marketing Skills: Cold Email

Targets cold outbound that gets replies without sounding like a sales machine. The writing principles: write as a peer rather than a vendor, make every sentence earn its place, connect personalization to a real problem, lead with their world, and make one low-friction ask. Separate sections handle voice, structure and subject lines. Beyond the copy, the description covers follow-up sequences, sending domains and warm-up, deliverability, LinkedIn touches, multichannel cadences and handling replies. It asks who you are writing to and what outcome you want before drafting anything.

## Use it to

- Draft a first-touch email to a named prospect with a problem-linked opening
- Build a follow-up sequence that adds a new angle each time instead of nudging
- Review a template that gets no replies and find what makes it sound generic
- Plan sending domains and warm-up before launching a campaign
