# Knowledge Work: Draft Outreach

Writes outreach grounded in your actual CRM and email context. It gathers context about the prospect, drafts the email, creates it as an email draft for review rather than sending it, and adds the contact to a sequence in a sales engagement tool only when asked. It handles cold emails, re-engagement and multi-touch sequences. The shared rules apply: ground field and stage names on the live CRM schema, cite values as read and show human labels. The draft-first default fits teams that want a person to approve every send.

## Use it to

- Draft a cold email to a named prospect as an unsent draft for review
- Re-engage a stalled opportunity using the history in the CRM
- Write a multi-touch sequence and enroll the contact once approved
- Keep personalization tied to facts found in connected systems
