# PM Skills: Market Sizing

Estimates the total addressable market, the serviceable addressable market and the serviceable obtainable market for a product. It uses both top-down and bottom-up estimation so the two can be compared, includes growth projections, and asks the model to think step by step through its analysis before presenting an output structure. Best practices close the skill. Use it when sizing an opportunity, estimating addressable market for investor pitches or evaluating market entry. It is compact, under six hundred words, and meant to be given a product description and any known figures.

## Use it to

- Size a new product's market for an investor pitch
- Cross-check a bottom-up estimate against a top-down one
- Project market growth alongside the current size
- Evaluate whether a market entry is worth pursuing
- Document the assumptions behind each figure so an investor can challenge them line by line
