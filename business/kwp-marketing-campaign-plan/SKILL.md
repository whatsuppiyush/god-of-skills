# Knowledge Work: Campaign Plan

Turns a marketing goal into an executable brief. The structure runs through campaign overview, target audience, key messages, channel strategy, a week-by-week content calendar with dependencies, the list of content pieces needed, success metrics, budget allocation if a budget is given, and risks with mitigations. Triggered by the /campaign-plan command or a request to plan or build a campaign, it fits product launches, lead-generation pushes and awareness campaigns. It belongs to Anthropic's marketing plugin and can use connected tools through that plugin's connector placeholders.

## Use it to

- Convert a launch goal into a campaign brief with a content calendar
- Allocate a stated budget across channels with reasoning
- List the content pieces a campaign needs and their dependencies
- Document risks and mitigations before committing spend
- Share the risks and mitigations section with legal and finance before the budget is approved

## How to use it

**Claude Code**
1. In a terminal, add the marketplace and install the plugin that holds this skill.
2. $ claude plugin marketplace add anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins
3. $ claude plugin install marketing@knowledge-work-plugins
