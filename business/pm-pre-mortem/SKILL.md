# PM Skills: Pre-Mortem

Imagines that a launch has failed and works backward. Risks are categorised as tigers (real problems), paper tigers (overblown concerns) and elephants (the unspoken worries nobody raised). Each is then classified as launch-blocking, fast-follow or track, so the team knows what to fix before shipping. The model acts as a veteran product manager running the exercise on a PRD or launch plan you pass in. It is about five hundred and seventy words, plus a context section and notes. Use it when preparing for launch or when you want to surface what could go wrong before the date is fixed.

## Use it to

- Run a pre-mortem on a PRD before engineering starts
- Separate real launch blockers from concerns that sound scary but are not
- Surface unspoken doubts the team has not voiced
- Build an action list sorted into must-fix and can-follow
