# ScrapeCreators Competitor Social Research

Combines profile data, recent posts, outlier analysis, transcripts and optionally comments to see what competitors are doing on social and what appears to work. The brief has an executive summary, a snapshot per competitor, what is working, cross-competitor patterns, gaps and opportunities, and recommended content tests. It lists the ScrapeCreators endpoints it uses and needs that service's API key. It suits teams researching a niche, comparing brands or creators, or turning public social data into a practical strategy brief rather than a data dump.

## Use it to

- Benchmark three competitors' social content in one brief
- Identify topics the whole niche ignores
- Write a list of content tests drawn from what is working
- Refresh a competitive social snapshot each quarter
- Present the gaps and opportunities section to a content team as a short planning memo

## How to use it

**Claude Code**
1. Install with the skills CLI.
2. $ npx skills add ScrapeCreators/social-media-research-skills
3. The skill calls the ScrapeCreators API, so set your key.
4. $ export SCRAPECREATORS_API_KEY=your_key
