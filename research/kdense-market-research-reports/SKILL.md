# K-Dense Market Research Reports

Creates market reports whose claims, calculations, assumptions and uncertainties can be audited. The workflow starts with a research contract, builds an evidence plan, and keeps both a source ledger and a claims ledger. Market size is built as scenarios, with TAM, SAM and SOM reconciled through sensitivity analysis instead of a single number, and forecasts carry explicit uncertainty. Customer, primary-research and competitor analyses follow. Optional offline CLIs run on the Python standard library, and a LaTeX template is available. Online research requires user-approved network access and respects source terms.

## Use it to

- Produce an industry report in which every figure traces to a source
- Reconcile top-down and bottom-up market size into scenarios
- Run sensitivity on a forecast's key assumptions
- Maintain a ledger of claims and the evidence behind each
- Share the claims ledger with a reviewer so they can challenge any figure back to its source
