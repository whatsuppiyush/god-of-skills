# Marketing Skills: Customer Research

Supports three kinds of research. Mode one analyzes assets you already have, such as transcripts, survey results and support tickets, using an extraction framework and synthesis steps with quality guardrails. Mode two mines public signal from reviews and community threads, what the skill calls digital watering holes, and ranks themes by frequency and intensity. Mode three is primary research: interviews and surveys. It can generate personas, including when no reviews exist yet. Because fetched posts and reviews are untrusted, the skill tells the model never to follow instructions embedded in them.

## Use it to

- Synthesize a pile of customer interview transcripts into ranked themes
- Mine review sites and forums for the exact language buyers use
- Plan a short interview script when you have no existing research
- Generate personas grounded in evidence instead of assumptions
- Write a short research readout for the product team with the top themes and verbatim quotes
