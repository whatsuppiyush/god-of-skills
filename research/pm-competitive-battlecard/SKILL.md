# PM Skills: Competitive Battlecard

Takes a single competitor name and builds a concise battlecard a salesperson can use mid-conversation. Contents are positioning against that competitor, a feature comparison, objection handling and win and loss patterns. It is meant for preparing sales teams, creating competitive material, or answering the question of why a prospect should not simply choose competitor X. At roughly four hundred words it is one of the shortest skills in the PM set, structured as context, instructions and further reading. The competitor is passed in as an argument, so the same skill can be run repeatedly for each rival.

## Use it to

- Produce a one-page battlecard for the competitor a rival most often faces
- Prepare answers to the question of why not the competitor
- Capture win and loss patterns in a shareable format
- Refresh battlecards for each rival on a regular schedule
