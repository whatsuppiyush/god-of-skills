# ScrapeCreators Comment Mining

Turns comment threads on public social posts and videos into a voice-of-customer report. It reads comments for questions, complaints, product ideas, misunderstandings and buying intent, and returns sections for summary, top themes, audience questions, objections and concerns, demand signals, exact language to reuse and content ideas. The data layer is the ScrapeCreators API, which requires its own key. Output is meant to feed product research, copywriting, objection handling and content planning. It is a compact skill of about five hundred words that sits within a broader set of social research workflows.

## Use it to

- Pull the objections out of comments on a competitor's launch video
- Collect exact customer phrases to reuse in copy
- Find unanswered questions that could become content
- Spot buying-intent comments on a creator's product posts
- Turn the exact-language section into a glossary your copywriters can paste from

## How to use it

**Claude Code**
1. Install with the skills CLI.
2. $ npx skills add ScrapeCreators/social-media-research-skills
3. The skill calls the ScrapeCreators API, so set your key.
4. $ export SCRAPECREATORS_API_KEY=your_key
