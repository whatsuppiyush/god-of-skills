# Anthropic Canvas Design

A two-step creative process. First the model names an aesthetic movement in a word or two and writes a few paragraphs on how it shows up in space, color, scale, rhythm and hierarchy. Then it paints that philosophy onto a canvas as a PNG or PDF where roughly nine tenths is visual and text is a minor accent. The instructions push hard on craftsmanship, asking that the finished piece look labored over by an expert, and warn against copying existing artists. A multi-page option extends one philosophy across a series. Output is limited to markdown, PDF and PNG files.

## Use it to

- Make a poster for an event where the visual identity matters more than the copy
- Generate a set of matching cover pages for a report series from one design philosophy
- Explore several distinct art directions for a brand mood board before committing
- Produce print-ready PDF artwork with minimal text for a gallery or zine
